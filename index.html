About Me

I am a Data Scientist (AI/ML) at Sephora. My work is in graph machine learning and recommendation, applied to drug repositioning in research and to personalization at Sephora.

I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of Cincinnati in 2023, advised by Dr. Anil Jegga. My dissertation used graph neural networks on multimodal biomedical data for drug repositioning and polypharmacy adverse event detection. During my PhD I also worked at Seagate on deep learning for manufacturing sensor data.
